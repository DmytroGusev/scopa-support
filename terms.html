Terms of use

Scopa: Riviera Cards is a recreational card game with no real-money wagers, advertising or purchases. All scores are game points without monetary value.

Offline play is available without a developer account. Optional online play uses Apple Game Center and depends on Apple services and the other players’ connections. Disconnecting ends the online session. Local saves can be lost if you delete the app or restore a device.

The application implements the documented classic Scopa rules. Traditional house rules may differ. Card illustrations and interface are original to this application.
