Scopa Capture Lab · Privacy · Terms

Privacy policy

Effective 4 November 2026. Scopa Capture Lab is developed by Dmytro Husiev. This policy describes build 2 and later. Earlier build 1 did not include Firebase Analytics or a developer-operated room directory.

Offline play and training

Saved games, preferences, training progress, statistics and the last 100 round analyses stay on your device. Device backups may include these files. Offline play does not require Game Center or analytics.

Online rooms

Apple Game Center authenticates players and transports match messages. Participating players see Game Center display names. The host device holds the game and checks commands. Each guest receives only its own hand and captured cards plus public table information; other hands and the undealt stock are not sent to guests.

Our separate Scopa room directory, hosted on Cloudflare Workers, verifies a signed Game Center team-player identifier and manages temporary room membership, logical seats, bot settings and readiness. The identifier is used for multiplayer functionality, not advertising. Public listings expose room IDs, seat counts, target score, bot settings, status and expiry; they do not expose player IDs, names or invitation capabilities. A private invitation contains a bearer capability: share it only with intended players.

The directory does not receive cards, moves, scores or Firebase identifiers. Waiting rooms expire after 15 minutes, or five minutes without the host; inactive guests expire after two minutes. Active rooms close when a member leaves or expires. Authentication sessions expire after one hour, signature replay records after five minutes, mutation retry records after two minutes. Expired records are pruned on requests and a periodic cleanup. Short-lived hashed network addresses support rate limiting. Cloudflare and Apple may process network and security information as infrastructure providers under their policies. Worker request logging is disabled.

Optional Firebase Analytics

Analytics is off by default. We configure the Firebase Analytics SDK only after you choose Allow analytics. Declining does not limit play, rooms, training or the journal. You can turn it off in Settings; the app disables collection and resets its local analytics identifier. This does not by itself erase data already received by Google.

If enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase processes an app-instance identifier, app interactions, general game configuration (2/3/4 seats and target score), application/device information and technical diagnostics. Google may derive approximate region from network information; precise location is not requested. We send a small fixed event set: offline and online starts, completed rounds, training attempts and solutions, and room creation/join. We do not send names, Game Center identifiers, room IDs, invitation links, hands, captured cards or moves to Firebase. Analytics identifiers are not joined to room-directory identities.

We use the Analytics SDK variant without advertising-ID support. IDFV collection, advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalization and automatic screen reporting are disabled. There are no ads, cross-company tracking or sales of personal information. Google processes analytics as our service provider. Analytics retention follows the settings of this app’s dedicated Google Analytics property; contact us for access or deletion requests and current retention details. See Firebase privacy information and Google’s privacy policy.

Your choices

Use Settings to change analytics consent. Deleting the app removes its local saves, journal, training progress and preferences, subject to backups you control. There is no developer account to register or delete. Manage Game Center in Apple system settings. You can contact us to exercise applicable data rights or ask a privacy question. For analytics deletion, do not send invitation capabilities or Game Center credentials.

Support and website

If you email support, your email and message are used to answer your request. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; it adds no analytics or cookies, while GitHub may process normal network/security information.
