Privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026. Scopa: Riviera Cards is developed by Dmytro Husiev.

Offline play

Saved games, preferences and statistics are stored on your device. We do not operate an application server, run advertising, use tracking or analytics SDKs, or sell personal information.

Online play

Apple Game Center manages authentication, player identity, invitations and transport. During a match, player IDs, display names and gameplay messages pass between participating devices. The host device holds the full game and all hands. Each guest receives only their own hand and public game information. We do not store this information on a developer server. Apple processes Game Center information under its own privacy policy.

Controls

You can play offline without Game Center. Deleting the app removes its local saves and preferences, subject to any device backup you control. You can manage Game Center in Apple system settings.

Support and website

If you email support, your email and message are used to answer your request. This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process normal request information. We do not add analytics or cookies.
