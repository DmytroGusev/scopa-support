Scopa: Riviera Cards

A bright Italian card table for iPhone and iPad. Play Scopa offline with 2–4 seats, three computer levels, capture previews, hints, undo and automatic saves. Four players form opposite partnerships.

Online games use Apple Game Center matchmaking and friend invitations. Everyone needs compatible app versions and matching player count and target. A disconnect stops the session; reconnection is not available.

Support

For help, email dmytro.gusev@gmail.com. Include app version, device model and the steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords or private keys.

Quick rules

Play one card to capture a matching table value, or a sum if no single match exists. Clear the table for a scopa, except on the final play. Score for cards, coins, settebello and primiera. Read the full localized rules inside the app.
