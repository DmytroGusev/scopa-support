Scopa Capture Lab

Classic Italian Scopa, with a studio for understanding every capture.

Play

Use a 40-card deck. Two or three players compete individually; four players form opposite-seat partnerships. Select a hand card and tap table cards to build an equal-value capture. Matching single cards take priority over sums. Targets: 11, 16 or 21 points.

Capture Lab and journal

Solve a daily UTC exercise or explore practice situations, compare all legal captures and understand single-card priority. The round journal keeps a score breakdown and a primiera lens for your own captured cards.

Online tables

Open Online tables and sign in to Game Center. Create a public or private table with 2–4 seats, or join a listed table. At least two humans are required; the host may fill remaining seats with bots. Each player taps Ready, then the host taps Start. Bot changes reset human readiness. Private tables require the full invitation link. Keep the host connected: a disconnect freezes play, and a new table is needed.
